# Product specification

## Mission
Learn → Remember → Use → Experience → Create → Reflect → Build → Next Adventure.

Serve a family in Chile, initially with anonymous profiles Child A (around 9) and Child B (under 6). Active device use should normally fit 10–15 minutes, with paper writing, speaking and observation away from the screen. A parent can reduce new content, pause it, or select review-only. No punitive streaks.

## First acceptance milestone
An installable APK for BOOX Nova Air C: parent sets a private PIN, two child profiles, today’s default five new characters, due review, several quiz modes, persisted completion and independent mastery. Entire flow works without a network. A PIN-protected local parent view shows both children’s completion. APK compilation is not evidence of BOOX hardware verification.

## Learning
Six stages: CHARACTER, WORD, SENTENCE, READING, EXPRESSION, CREATION. Track character, vocabulary, reading, writing, pronunciation, expression, real-world usage, creator and builder levels independently. Initial content policy focuses on characters; higher-stage runtime content remains gated until appropriate reviewed content exists.

Recognition, pronunciation, meaning and writing are distinct evidence. Self-assessment has less weight than an adult test. Exposure is not mastery. Wrong answers shorten review intervals without erasing history. No inferred speech or handwriting recognition.

## Rewards and seasons
Daily Stamp represents effort. Weekly Choice Ticket is a real-life choice. Parent-tested Master Stars and cooperative Family Stars support configurable real-life rewards. Hidden budget remains parent-only. Wishes require parent approval; no purchase automation. Travel is a family decision, never payment for learning. Season data is narrative progress; learning data is lifetime progress.

## Growth
Travel memories are family-private source material. Creator skills grow from stable framing to Chinese storytelling. Builder Lab develops idea articulation, testing and judgment, with Keep / Change / No and an enduring portfolio. Child prototypes must be isolated and reviewed by parents before merging.

## Explicit MVP exclusions
Cloud account creation, public publishing, live AI chat, handwriting recognition, AI vision, travel booking, purchases, media editing and child production code access. These cannot delay the first APK.

## Child-requested creative preview

After the first real-use milestone, the family requested color and participatory visual explanations. Optional coloring and finite tap-stepped scenes extend Learn → Create → Reflect while preserving the daily lesson and review model. They do not award mastery/stamps or require every child to finish every scene. No autoplay or animation timers are introduced; brush movement follows deliberate input. Children can choose Keep / Change / No about their prototype. Paper writing and real-world speaking remain central, and each child's local work is separate.
